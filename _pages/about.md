My name is Xiaocao Ouyang (欧阳小草). I am currently an Associate Professor at the School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence, Southwestern University of Finance and Economics. I obtained my Ph.D. degree at Southwest Jiaotong University, supervised by Prof. Yan Yang. She also works closely with Prof. Tianrui Li.

My main research interests lie in

- Urban Computing
- Spatio-Temporal Data Mining
- Spatio-Temporal Transfer Learning
- Federated Spatio-Temporal Learning
- Large Language Models
